Start from intended capabilities and role outcomes. Research discoverable facts before
owner questions. Compare native reuse, configuration, adaptation and named custom gaps.

decision at a time; do not re-ask accepted scope or require arbitrary research passes.

separately. Prototypes are conditional and require execution authorization. No source
runtime was executed during this bootstrap. Keep technical proof distinct from human UAT.
